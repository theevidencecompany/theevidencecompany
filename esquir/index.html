Revolutionising systematic reviews

Systematic reviews & meta-analyses still feel like scaling a mountain by hand.

Fragmented databases, bespoke syntaxes, near-duplicate hits, obscure synonyms, painful deduplication, thousands of abstracts, clumsy extraction, manual double-checking, and subjective bias assessment with no reliable automation.

The SRMA Mountain (Problem)

Weeks or months of repetitive work—slows discovery, inflates costs, and risks missing critical evidence.

  • Disparate databases & bespoke search syntaxes
  • Hundreds of near-identical hits
  • Hunting down obscure synonyms
  • Painstakingly weeding out duplicates
  • Poring over thousands of abstracts
  • Clumsy data-extraction tools
  • Manual double-checking
  • Subjective, error-prone bias assessments with no reliable automation

Our Goal

Tackle fragmentation and manual grind, unifying the workflow.

Our Solution — An End-to-End Engine

Introducing a PICO-driven, semi-automated workflow.

Unified Search

Single PubMed-style query translates to optimized searches across PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, Cochrane, and Embase.

Automatic synonym and controlled-vocabulary expansion (e.g., MeSH to Emtree).

API-driven queries. Real-time total and deduplicated counts.

Accelerated Screening

AI-powered screening suggestions.

PICO-based keyword filters.

Structured Data Extraction

Less busywork, more insight.

Scientific Validity & Feasibility

  • Query translation: rule-based tables (MeSH to Emtree) peer-reviewed by information specialists.
  • Deduplication: tuned and validated against gold-standard reference sets.
  • Screening models: calibrated on thousands of human-labeled abstracts.
  • Data extraction: accuracy aligns within 95% of expert consensus.
  • Early prototypes matched or exceeded manual workflows in speed and fidelity.

FAQ

Does the search query generator produce queries for PubMed, Embase, Scopus, the Cochrane Library, and Web of Science?

Yes—the search query generator produces database-specific queries for PubMed, Embase, Scopus, the Cochrane Library, and Web of Science from a PICO-based workflow.

Is Esqui.R only a search tool?

No—Esqui.R is a gateway suite with additional modules for screening, risk of bias, and extraction coming soon.
